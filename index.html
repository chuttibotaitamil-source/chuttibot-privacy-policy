Privacy Policy — Chuttibot Browser Pro

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chuttibot Browser Pro handles information when you use the Android browser. For questions, contact info@commoneservices.online.

1. User accounts

No account or login is required to use Chuttibot Browser Pro. Websites visited through the browser may require separate accounts.

2. Browsing information

The browser may process or store browsing history, visited URLs, bookmarks, preferences, download records, cookies, cache, and website storage on your device to provide browser functions. Websites you visit may independently collect information under their own privacy policies.

3. Incognito mode

Incognito mode is intended to reduce retention of browsing history. It does not make you anonymous online, and it is not a guarantee that all cookies or temporary data are erased. Websites, internet service providers, and network administrators may still observe browsing activity.

4. Downloads

Downloaded files may be saved to your device. You can manage them using the browser's available controls or your device's file manager. Delete downloaded files separately when necessary.

5. Ad blocking and advertising

The browser includes an ad-blocking feature that may reduce some advertisements or tracking requests, but it cannot block every ad or tracker. An ad blocker does not mean that the app itself displays advertising. If advertising services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe their relevant data practices.

6. Permissions

Depending on the features used and Android version, the browser may request access to storage or file selection, camera, microphone, location, or notifications. You can manage permissions in Android settings. Permissions support applicable browser or website functions.

7. Third-party websites

Websites opened in the browser may use their own cookies, analytics, advertising, or other technologies. Their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Security

We aim to apply reasonable safeguards to browser functionality and user information, but no app or internet connection can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored browser information may remain until removed using available browser controls or Android settings. Downloaded files may need to be deleted separately. Clearing browser history does not delete information independently collected by websites.

10. Children's privacy

This is a general-purpose browser and is not specifically designed for children. Appropriate supervision and applicable age requirements should be followed.

11. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as features, data practices, or requirements change. The updated date will be revised accordingly.
